Rural West Project

The brand

Logos, ten colors in four groups, Oswald and Lato, and the tokens that put them on screen in light and dark. Everything on this page is drawn from the same tokens.css and tokens.json you can download below.

Logos

Use the SVG on the web; it scales to any size. The SVGs are traced from the original PNGs and match them to within half a pixel. The files don't switch with the theme: pick black for light grounds and white for dark ones. Never redraw, recolor, stretch or add effects.

Brand palette

The ten colors from the brand guide, in its four groups. They never change with the theme. The partisan colors encode party only, always with an R or D, and never as buttons, links or decoration.

Screen colors

The tokens to build with. Each one is shown in both themes, whichever theme this page is in. Every text color holds at least 4.5:1 on the grounds its note names.

Election data

The conventions the 2026 election site used for results. Party color always means the party of whoever's winning. RWP's own outcome is a word or icon, never color alone.

Type

Oswald for headlines, Lato for everything else, in the guide's weights only: Oswald 600, 700 and 900; Lato 200, 300, 400, 500 and 600. There's no Lato Bold, so Semibold is the emphasis weight. In CSS, each style is a class: .rwp-display, .rwp-body and so on.

Spacing & radii

A 4 px spacing scale and crisp corners. The brand is flat: its only shadows are the focus ring and the change highlight.

Files

To use the tokens on a site, copy tokens.css and the fonts/ folder together, then set data-theme="light" or "dark" on <html>. Load the fonts from these files, not Google Fonts, whose Lato and Oswald lack the guide's weights.
